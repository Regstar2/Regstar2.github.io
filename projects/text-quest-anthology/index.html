> The story

Zavalinka

A horror story told through pages of text and decisions. Choices affect which scenes become available, the characters' circumstances and the ending.

01 / story

A branching narrative

The story is written in Ink. Decisions lead down different paths, and reaching the end does not mean every outcome has been seen.

02 / progression

Continue where you stopped

Progress and discovered endings are stored on the device. You can restart a completed playthrough without erasing the endings you have already found.

> gameplay

What you can play

The MVP ships with one complete story and the reading features needed to finish it.

01READ

Page-based reading

Read the story page by page, with a text size setting that repaginates the content.

02CHOICE

Decisions and consequences

Make choices at story branches and reach different terminal endings.

03SAVE

Local progress

Resume after closing the application. Read progress is stored on the device.

04OFFLINE

Offline story

Read, make choices, continue and revisit endings without a required internet connection.

05LIBRARY

Story library

Choose a story from the built-in catalogue. The current MVP includes Zavalinka.

06RESET

Play again

Start a new playthrough while retaining the record of discovered endings.

> stack / scope

Built for Android

React Native and TypeScript power the app. Ink/inkjs handles the interactive narrative, with stories packaged in the application.

Current scope

Public MVP, not a finished anthology

The current release contains one story in Russian. There are no user accounts, cloud saves, browser version or iOS build. The main story works offline; the optional advertising SDK can use the network.

> data

Privacy and installation

Reading progress, settings and unlocked endings stay in local app storage. The Android build includes Yandex Mobile Ads, which can process technical data when ads load. Privacy policy ↗
